Why Restroom Cleanliness Matters

It's more than just an inconvenience; it affects public health, economics, and social equity.

Public Health Hazard

Unsanitary conditions are breeding grounds for pathogens, contributing to the spread of infectious diseases. Lack of hygiene facilities impacts community well-being directly.

Dignity and Equity

Inadequate restrooms disproportionately affect vulnerable populations, including the elderly, pregnant individuals, people with disabilities, and the unhoused.

Damaged Tourism & Business

Poor public amenities deter tourists and shoppers. Investing in clean facilities boosts local business confidence and improves the overall quality of life.

The Standard We Deserve

Our advocacy demands a shift from neglect to responsibility. We believe in four core pillars for a better public restroom infrastructure.

  • **Mandatory Daily Cleaning:** Scheduled, documented deep cleaning in all public facilities.

  • **Required Supplies:** Consistent stocking of soap, toilet paper, and working hand dryers or paper towels.

  • **Accessibility Checks:** Ensuring ADA compliance and safe, well-lit spaces for everyone.
